# Permanent research principles

1. One persistent developing neural learner per continuing experimental arm. Track
   identity and every explicit reset; environment resets do not erase knowledge.
2. Learn from environmental consequences. Never present a hand-coded policy or oracle
   as acquired intelligence. Keep sensory evidence separate from hidden evaluator truth.
3. No permanent architectural ceilings across model size, topology, memory, interfaces,
   cooperation or learning algorithm. Every current limit belongs to a versioned experiment.
4. Finite compute, memory, time, interaction and update budgets. External interruption,
   reset and rollback remain available; no simulated objective governs real resources.
5. Independent evaluation on unused worlds, matched controls, retained competence and
   resource accounting. No promotion from engineering tests, reward curves or stored weights.
6. Reproducible source, configuration, seeds, RNG, full learning state, lineage and logs.
   Preserve negative results and immutable recovery checkpoints.
7. Evidence-driven, reversible changes; no unrestricted real-world autonomy, deployment,
   source rewriting, persistence or compute acquisition.
