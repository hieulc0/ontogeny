# Vision

Ontogeny asks whether a neural organism can develop useful intelligence through its
own consequences, rather than repeatedly being replaced with task specialists.
We begin with one cell because learning, persistent state, partial sensing and real
simulator consequences already pose a falsifiable challenge. More cells would obscure
which mechanism caused improvement.

A digital cell is a neural perception/prediction/action interface, internal memory and
persistent learning state within an externally allocated budget. It is not a literal
biological cell. We make no claim of consciousness or biological life.

Developing intelligence means improving generalization, retaining knowledge, adapting
to changed rules and using experience to acquire later abilities more efficiently.
Reactive behavior can be useful without learning: our fixed resource-seeking heuristic
illustrates this. Learned behavior requires controlled evidence that updating from
experience improves future actions relative to matched unchanged controls.

Continual learning preserves and extends competence across successive encounters.
Learning-to-learn additionally improves future acquisition efficiency due to prior
experience; faster training loss alone does not establish that ability. Memory storage
alone does not establish either.

Interaction links representation to observable consequences and allows the organism's
actions to affect future evidence. Reward remains a training design, not an intelligence
measure. Prediction improvement and viable behavior must be independently assessed.
Multicellular communication and differentiation are hypotheses: coordination may cost
more than enlarging one learner. Capacity-matched comparisons must justify them.

No architecture defines intelligence permanently. Parameters, layers, dimensions,
heads, connections, topology, embeddings, contexts, memories, modules, input/output
representations, cell count and learning algorithms remain revisable hypotheses.
Every revision requires a finite resource envelope, independent evidence, migration
invariants and reversible checkpoints. Growth is permission to investigate, not a goal.


## One neural foundation, two experience paths

Mini-GPT is the internal canonical neural engine. Independent knowledge/reasoning
students and the embodied organism use the same implementation with distinct persistent
LearningState histories. Controlled consolidation is a research question: independently
learned changes must preserve the organism's prior capabilities under a preregistered
evaluation, never automatically overwrite its state. Structural development, memory
organization and learning-to-learn remain evidence-driven future work. Source consolidation
does not improve any historical learning verdict or make a scientific stage automatic.
